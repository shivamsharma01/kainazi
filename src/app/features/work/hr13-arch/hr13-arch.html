The console, from anywhere
Owner

Checks members, reports, and who is in the gym, without being on the floor.

Staff at the desk

Registers a member, takes the photo, and records the payment.

Web app

Dashboard, members, attendance, payments, and reports. React, served from Cloudflare.

Encrypted connection over the internet
Cloud
API

Members, memberships, payments, attendance, and the instruction to update each gate. Spring Boot.

Database

The record of members, payments, and attendance. MySQL.

The gym computer connects out to the cloud
At the gym
Gateway

A Windows service on a PC in the gym. It sends member changes to the gates and brings attendance back.

Local queue

Holds work that could not be delivered, so a power cut or a dropped connection does not lose it.

The gym network, to the gates
The gates
Entry

TrueFace3000. Stores the face, the name, and the validity dates, and opens the turnstile. Keeps working if the internet drops.

Exit

The same kind of gate, with its own copy of the members. Kept in step with the entry gate, and also fine offline.

A new member

Saved in the cloud, sent to both gates, and checked there before the console shows it as done.

A visit

The gate scans the face and stores the punch. The gym computer collects it, and the dashboard updates.

Internet down

The gates keep using the last update. Pending changes and punches catch up when the connection returns.
