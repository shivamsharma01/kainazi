About the Client

Meet HR13 Gym

HR13 Gym is one of Bahadurgarh's largest commercial fitness centres — a premium, professionally-run gym spread across 350–400 sq. yards, equipped with modern machines, treadmills, and a full cardio floor.

The gym owner had already made a smart investment in biometric entry: two TrueFace3000 face-recognition turnstile gates (by TimeWatch) at the gym entrance and exit. Each machine has a small camera screen that scans a member's face and either opens or blocks the turnstile based on stored validity dates. The idea was completely right — make gym entry secure, automatic, and professional.

The Problem

When Good Hardware Meets Broken Workflows

The hardware was there. The vision was right. But as membership grew past 1,200 people, the cracks in day-to-day management became serious business problems.

The Root of the Problem — 4 Records Per Page on a Tiny Screen

This photograph shows the actual TrueFace3000 screen — roughly the size of a smartphone. The "User List" screen shows only 4 records per page: Mintu Dhankhar (ID 147), Ashish Dalal (149), Shivam Gulia (150), and Vibhor Goel (165). The pagination at the bottom reads "284 / 303" — meaning the person is on page 284 out of 303 total pages, and a finger is visibly pressing the back-navigation button. With 1,200+ members, finding a specific person and changing their validity dates meant scrolling through hundreds of such tiny pages — slow, exhausting, and very prone to mistakes.

Problem 01

Managing 1,200+ Records on a Tiny Screen

The hardware screen shows only 4–5 records per page. Finding one member from 1,200+ required scrolling through hundreds of pages — slow, frustrating, error-prone. With 303 pages to navigate, finding one person could take 10–15 minutes.

Problem 02

Two Machines, Zero Automatic Sync

Entry and Exit devices were fully independent. Any change had to be manually repeated on both machines. If the owner forgot, devices went out of sync — members were blocked at one gate but let through the other.

Problem 03

iAS Software — On-Premise Only & Still Limited

TimeWatch's own iAS software required the owner to be physically present at the gym. It also didn't support uploading a member's face photo from software into the device — the most critical operation for new registrations.

Problem 04

Business Records on Paper & Registers

Memberships, payment history, and renewal dates were maintained in physical files and registers. Tracking expired memberships, overdue payments, and upcoming renewals was a full-time manual job with no visibility.

Problem 05

Owner Couldn't Manage Remotely

The owner runs multiple businesses. When away for 3–4 days, tasks piled up — new registrations waited, renewals were delayed. The whole business operation depended on his physical presence at the gym.

Problem 06

No Reminders for Inactive or Expiring Members

Members whose memberships expired would simply stop coming — the gym had no way to know or follow up. No WhatsApp reminders, no personalised offers to re-engage members who hadn't visited in 3+ months.

The core pain in one sentence: A gym doing serious business with 1,200+ members was still being run with the operational efficiency of a small neighbourhood shop — manual, offline, slow, and completely dependent on the owner being physically present.
Our Approach

Design First. Then Build.

Before writing a single line of code, we spent time at the gym, understood the hardware deeply, and mapped out every business workflow — from new member registration to membership expiry to device synchronisation.

We discovered early that the TrueFace3000 hardware is powerful — it has a full SDK (Software Development Kit) that allows external software to communicate with it over the gym's local network. This was the key insight: rather than fighting the hardware, we could build a software layer that speaks directly to it and automates everything that was being done manually.

Our approach was to build a three-layer system — a cloud web app the owner uses from anywhere, a cloud backend that is the single source of truth, and a small on-premise gateway that bridges the cloud with the physical biometric machines:

Under the Hood

The Invisible Magic — Automatic Device Sync

This is the most technically complex — and most valuable — part of the entire project. It is what makes this solution fundamentally different from any existing off-the-shelf gym software.

Most gym management software manages members in a database. But the moment you close the app, the physical gate knows nothing about it. We built a real-time synchronisation engine that keeps the cloud database and both physical biometric devices in perfect sync at all times — automatically.

🔄

Desired-State Synchronisation — Write, Verify, Confirm

When a member is added or renewed in the app, the backend calculates the exact "desired state" for each device — the full user record, face photo, and validity dates that should be on that machine. The gateway writes this to the physical device, reads it back to verify it was stored correctly, and only marks the sync as SYNCHRONIZED after the read-back matches perfectly. If it doesn't match, it retries automatically. You are always shown the true state — never a fake success.

📡

Works Even When Internet Goes Down

The biometric devices are standalone — they store everything locally. If the internet goes down or the cloud server is unreachable, the devices continue working normally based on the last synced data. Members are never stuck at the gate because of a network outage. When the connection is restored, the gateway automatically syncs any pending changes — no manual intervention needed, no data loss.

🏃

Automatic Validity Push on Every Renewal

When a membership is renewed, the new validity end date is automatically calculated and pushed to both Entry and Exit devices. The system sends the full user record on every update — protecting against a known hardware firmware behaviour where a partial update could accidentally clear validity dates and open access to everyone.

🧊

Freeze / Unfreeze a Member Instantly

Need to suspend a member's access? One click in the web app freezes their account and pushes a "frozen" status to both physical devices immediately. Their face will no longer open the turnstile. Unfreezing restores access just as instantly. No need to touch the hardware at all.

👁️

Live Attendance Without Any Manual Entry

Every face scan creates an attendance record automatically. The system polls the device's attendance log every 15 seconds and pulls the latest punches — with exact timestamp, which gate (Entry/Exit), direction (In/Out), and whether access was GRANTED or DENIED. These appear live on the dashboard and in each member's profile. Zero manual attendance entry. Zero human error.
